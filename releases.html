Release notes

What changed in each version of WiFi Scan. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 23 November 2026Latest

  • First release. A WiFi and network analyser that tells you why your WiFi is slow and how to fix it.
  • Network health score, speed test with delay under load, packet loss and lag grade, your package checked, history and sharing.
  • Live signal and graphs, Signal Finder with beeps and vibration, 4G and 5G readings and mobile data tests.
  • Channel charts for 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz with channel ratings, the best channel, and router guides for BT, EE, Plusnet, Sky, Virgin Media, TalkTalk and Vodafone.
  • Devices on your WiFi with names, makers, labels and new-device alerts, and the Bluetooth list with a proximity finder.
  • Toolbox: ping, traceroute, DNS lookup and DNS speed test, port scanner for your own devices, Wake-on-LAN, whois, public IP, IP calculator and MTU test.
  • Security check, Why is my WiFi slow?, floor-plan and camera-walk heatmaps and saved places.
  • Scheduled tests with alerts for slow speed, high delay, outages and new devices, PDF and CSV reports, widgets, a Quick Settings tile and encrypted backups.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called WiFiScan.apk. It is 3.5 MB (3,521,855 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

46377f1442adff6e25751275246907d2333f382a20db9439214af9173e1a2a22

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile WiFiScan.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
