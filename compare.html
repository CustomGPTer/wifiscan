What WiFi Scan does not do

WiFi Scan cannot change your router's settings for you; it tells you what to change and opens your router's page. It has no remote access to your network from outside, and it is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the others do more

WiFiman connects to Ubiquiti's own UniFi equipment and includes a remote-access VPN for it. Network Analyzer supports IPv6 throughout and more DNS record types. Both install and update from Google Play.
